**General Description:**
In this project, we will create a website in the style of an old-fashioned arcade machine, 
complete with playable game(s), a high score leaderboard, and a retro 80s design. 
The website will invoke nostalgia and provide a fun and accessible format to play arcade games. 

Our application will support both dynamic and static data. Along with this, we will have a secure login system. 
Users will be able to log in and see the highest scores (top 10 or so). If they score enough points in the game, 
their score will be added to the leaderboard.

**Technologies/Libraries:**
Canvas API will be the core of our website, used for game creation
TypeScript for better error detection, tooling, and maintenance purposes
MongoDB for storing the scores and user login data
Passport.js and bcryptjs for user login implementation
Express-session for cookies
Express for the server
Render for deployment

**Project Ideation:**
The goal of our project is to invoke that feeling of nostalgia that comes with playing an arcade game on an arcade machine. 
We want to give our users a sense of escape – a break from the day-to-day – to sit back and enjoy a game of Galaga.
Our artistic vision of the retro/80s feel aims to keep the style alive. 
This will also target a competitive audience where players try to get high scores on the leaderboard.
